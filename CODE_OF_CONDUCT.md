# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participation in this project should remain professional, constructive, and focused on improving the software and its community.

Contributors are expected to:

- communicate respectfully
- discuss technical disagreements in good faith
- provide actionable and evidence-based feedback
- respect different levels of experience
- keep reviews focused on the contribution rather than the contributor
- avoid harassment, personal attacks, intimidation, discrimination, or deliberately disruptive behavior
- respect privacy and security-sensitive information

## Technical Discussions

Disagreement about architecture, implementation, performance, security, or project direction is welcome when it remains constructive.

Claims about correctness, security, compatibility, or performance should be supported by reproducible evidence whenever practical.

## Security-Sensitive Information

Potential vulnerabilities and sensitive security information must not be used to threaten, pressure, or expose other participants.

Follow the private disclosure process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, reject, lock, or remove contributions and interactions that materially violate these standards.

Repeated or serious violations may result in restrictions on participation in the project.

## Scope

These expectations apply to participation in this repository and project-related collaboration spaces controlled by the project.
